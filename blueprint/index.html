Order Intake Process

  1. 1. Inquiry Received
  2. 2. Discovery Questions
    (Type of product, theme, colors, name/logo, date needed, pickup/delivery/shipping preference)
  3. 3. Quote Provided
    (Include production timeframe, policy, deposit amount)
  4. 4. Invoice Sent
  5. 5. Deposit Received
    (Secures space on production calendar)
  6. 6. Design Mockup Created
    (Layout digital elements based on customer brief)
  7. 7. Design Approval
    (Draft sent to client -> Client confirms/requests edits -> Final approval locked)
  8. 8. Materials Procurement
    (Verify inventory; source blanks, specific sub inks, or paper if needed)
  9. 9. Production
    (Print design, align and heat-tape substrates, press product at designated times/temps)
  10. 10. Quality Check
    (Inspect for faded spots, ghosting, alignment issues, or surface flaws)
  11. 11. Packaging
    (Clean item, wrap carefully, add care card/logo sticker, box/bag up)
  12. 12. Shipping/Delivery (limited)/Pickup
    (Collect remaining balance, send tracking details, or confirm pickup location)

Deposit Policy & Safeguards

  • 💰 Under $100: Full payment upfront before production begins

  • 💵 $100–$500: 50% deposit minimum required to start

  • 💳 Over $500: 60% deposit minimum required to start

  • 🍶 ***Custom Products: ALL deposits are strictly non-refundable once materials are ordered or design drafts are completed.***

Inventory Targets & Triggers

🔄 Weekly Inventory Sweeps: Conduct manual audits every Sunday afternoon to catch material usage spikes before production delays happen. 🚨 Baseline Safety Thresholds: Trigger automatic restock orders immediately when standard blanks (including 20oz skinny tumblers, 11oz/15oz ceramic mugs, and clear acrylic spray bottles) fall to 5 units or fewer. 🎨 Consumables Warning: Reorder specialized sublimation inks, transfer papers, and thermal tape once active supplies hit 25% capacity remaining. ⚠️ Bulk Requests Rule: For large custom corporate, school, or church orders, never pull raw stock from baseline inventory; all target materials must be ordered independently after the client's deposit has completely cleared.

Target Bank Account Matrix

🏦 Checking Account: Main operating hub. Receives all client deposits and invoice payments. Disburses baseline supply costs. 💰 Savings Account: General stability holding. Holds the 10% business allocation cushion. 🚨 Emergency Account: Funded continuously until 3–6 months of fixed business overhead is securely locked. 🛑 Tax Savings Account: Holds the 10% tax withholding. Untouchable until quarterly or annual tax filings. 🛠️ Equipment Savings Account: Receives the 5% allocation. Dedicated exclusively to major assets (such as purchasing new equipment).
